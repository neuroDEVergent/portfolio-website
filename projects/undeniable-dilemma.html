About the project

I started this channel because I was just bored honestly... It started with just expressing my opinions on random topics with some gameplay footage in the background. But it just felt really low effort and I was just dipping my toes into this YouTube thing.

So I tried making more educational oriented videos, talking about stuff I learned about. It would require me to research, fact check, form a concise script with a singular direction. And then make a small project, record it, animate and come up with creative ideas. Honestly I just loved the whole process and I definetely plan on making a bunch more!

I also built a skill in editing audio and video! Not only that, but I stumbled upon this super cool Python library called Manim, which I use to make most of the visual mathematical explanations in videos. It can do some cool things and I'm so glad I found it!

As you might have noticed from surfing my website (I honestly doubt anyone actually reads any of this), maaaaybe, just maybe, you have noticed this little habbit of mine, of BEING STRONGLY OPINIONATED. So to keep the momentum going, I think I'll also post a bunch more videos where I just talk about some experiences that I've had, or some thoughts that I have on certain topics. I'd like to get more into expressing myself, my beliefs and what I stand for.
